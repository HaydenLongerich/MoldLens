Directory terms & privacy

The MoldLens directory helps you find mold contractors. Google listings and reviews do not mean a company is subscribed, licensed, insured or endorsed by MoldLens. Search results can be incomplete. Distances are approximate straight-line distances between postal-code locations and business locations.

Google business content is subject to the Google Terms of Service and Google Maps Additional Terms, incorporated into these directory terms.

Your information

Searching sends your postal code to Google to locate nearby businesses. Google's handling of that information is explained in the Google Privacy Policy, incorporated into this directory privacy notice. MoldLens stores Google business IDs and fetches business ratings and review excerpts when you request them; it does not keep a permanent copy of Google reviews.

When you choose to share a screening, subscribed contractors within 50 km can review the whole project and submit proposals. Invitations go to their signup email. Your account-free contact email and phone are shared with the contractor when you request follow-up or select its quote. Contractor signup email, postal code, preferences and invitation delivery records are stored to provide this service. Contractors can stop invitations in workspace settings. Local demo memberships do not charge money.

Use your contractor workspace to manage quote invitations and your customer screenings page to manage saved screenings. Removing a screening hides it from your list; it does not erase existing quotes or messages.
